# Privacy Policy – Lurk Report

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Lurk Report does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

## What is stored
The extension stores the names of Twitch channels you watch and how long you watched them per day. This data is saved locally in your browser using `chrome.storage.local`.

## What is not done
- No data is sent to any server.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising.
- No account or login is required.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the extension deletes all stored data.
